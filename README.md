### 🥊 Fight Club Online — Real-Time Multiplayer Fighting Platform

**Description:**
Fight Club Online is a real-time multiplayer gaming platform powered by a microservices architecture, featuring AI-driven voice chat moderation, matchmaking, and social lobby management.

**Problem Addressed:**
Multiplayer platforms often suffer from poor real-time performance, disorganized lobbies, and the lack of intelligent moderation in voice communication.

**Solution:**
Designed a microservices-based system using Clean Architecture to ensure scalability and maintainability, integrating AI-powered voice chat for real-time interaction and moderation.

**My Contributions:**
- Designed and implemented the **microservices architecture** using Clean Architecture principles.
- Developed the **AI-powered voice chat system** in TypeScript for real-time moderation.
- Built the **WebSocket communication engine** managing matchmaking logic, social lobbies, and private rooms for concurrent users.
- Led integration of real-time features under tight deadlines through close team coordination.

**Technologies:**
`Java` · `TypeScript` · `Node.js` · `WebSockets` · `Microservices` · `Clean Architecture` · `AI Voice` · `CSS3`

### 🩺 SRPHS — Intelligent Metabolic Risk Prevention System

**Description:**
SRPHS is a preventive health platform powered by Artificial Intelligence that analyzes lifestyle habits to predict metabolic risk and generate personalized health recommendations.

**Problem Addressed:**
Many people lack early warnings about metabolic risk due to the absence of accessible, data-driven preventive tools.

**Solution:**
Built an ML-powered system using Random Forest models to predict risk, SHAP for explainability, and Gemini AI to generate personalized health guidance.

**My Contributions:**
- Implemented **predictive metabolic risk models** using Random Forest (Scikit-Learn).
- Integrated **SHAP** for transparent and explainable AI predictions.
- Connected **Gemini AI** to generate personalized health recommendations.
- Designed and managed the **MongoDB Atlas** data storage layer.

**Technologies:**
`Python` · `Scikit-Learn` · `SHAP` · `Gemini AI` · `MongoDB Atlas`

### 🚗 RideECI — University Ride-Sharing Platform

**Description:**
RideECI is a platform designed to improve mobility for university students and staff by enabling secure ride coordination within the university community.

**Problem Addressed:**
University members often face inefficient and uncoordinated transportation, with no secure system to organize shared rides or communicate safely with other users.

**Solution:**
Developed a ride-sharing platform integrating event-driven microservices, asynchronous communication queues, and containerized deployment with high availability.

**My Contributions:**
- Designed and implemented the **Administration microservice** and the **Notifications module** under Hexagonal Architecture.
- Configured **asynchronous communication** through event queues ensuring high availability.
- Deployed and managed the system using **Kubernetes** for container orchestration.
- Ensured seamless integration between REST APIs and database layers.

**Technologies:**
`Spring Boot` · `TypeScript` · `REST APIs` · `PostgreSQL` · `MongoDB` · `Kubernetes`

**Repository:** [📁 View on GitHub](https://github.com/RIDECI/ATENEA_ADMINISTRATION_BACKEND)
